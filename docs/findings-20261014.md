# The CHTD experiments: what was asked, what was found, what remains

Written 2026-10-14 as the project's single entry point. Every number below is computed from records
checked into `runs/` and is reproducible with the scripts named beside it. This document states the
result the way the evidence supports it, lists what the evidence does **not** support, and names
what a next run would have to change.

**The result in one paragraph.** The method — co-harness distillation on the student's own states,
with the teacher's decision as the label — was built, frozen, and run end to end: three held-out
task sets, up to three training seeds per arm, every answer hand-read, and the instruments
themselves calibrated against two independent readers. What the project can defend is a **negative
result with a measured reason**: at the data scale that was actually run (9 student-state rows), the
paired contrast the design exists to measure — student-state data vs teacher-own data — is **not
distinguishable at 30 tasks**: the observed difference is 0–3 pp against a 26.6 pp detection floor
(`eval/power_holdout3.py`). The only arm in the whole batch whose effect approaches the floor is
`v5` (93 teacher-demonstration rows, −8 tasks / −27 pp against the base), and v5 is not the
student-state mechanism. The fine-tunes also proved **unstable in ways that are themselves a
finding**: the same nine-row recipe moved whole task sets between seeds, and a policy regression
appeared under a changed prompt. Neither instability is caused by the CHTD data structure; both are
properties of nine rows trained with the loss on the final answer.

## 1. The question and the design

The design is specified in [design.md](design.md) and the co-harness machinery in
[pipeline.md](pipeline.md). In the form the experiments test it:

- **Co-harness**: teacher and student run the *same frozen track* (byte-frozen preset
  `minimal-rl`; a drifted track fails to load). The only variable between arms is the model, then
  the data.
- **Targeted distillation**: the student runs first; the teacher is dropped into the student's
  states through a fork; where they diverge, the teacher's decision becomes the label. Nine
  single-decision rows, `supervise='last'` — this is the design's letter: one label per row.
- **The deciding comparison**: student-state rows vs teacher-own rows — same tasks, same recipe,
  same budget, same evaluation — differing only in *whose exploration precedes the decision*.
- **Evaluation**: fresh held-out diagnosis tasks nobody had read, run through
  `eval/holdout_sweep.py` against real serving endpoints, scored by a phrase gate and, on the
  record sets, by a full human read.

Two cross-cutting analyses were added after the third pass and are part of the result:

- **Instrument benchmarks** (`eval/scorer_benchmark_holdout3.py`): the phrase gate agrees with the
  author's 210-cell ledger at **75.7 %**, and with the 76-cell double-reader-agreed panel at
  **75.0 %**. The two human readers agree with each other on **84 %** of cells — the gate sits just
  under the readers' own reliability, and its remaining error is not phrase-fixable (a test-blame
  candidate catches 2 of 35 false positives; the rest are right-words-wrong-conclusion answers).
- **Power** (`eval/power_holdout3.py`): with the measured discordance rate π_d = 0.292, the
  minimum detectable effect at 30 tasks/arm is **26.6 pp (8 tasks)**; 10 pp needs ~227 tasks/arm.
  The observed student-state-vs-control differences (0–3 pp; pooled 95 % CI −7.6 to +10.9 pp over
  120 pair-cells) cannot carry a claim in either direction.

## 3. What the evidence supports

1. **The pipeline and the design are real.** A frozen co-harness track, byte-checked at load; a
   provenance gate that refuses mismatched arms; per-run track hashes on every record; an
   evaluation sweep that produces a ledger per session. Everything downstream recomputes from
   checked-in records (`eval/`, `harness/`, `runs/`).
2. **The paired question is not answerable at the scale run.** Student-state vs teacher-own: 0–3 pp
   observed against a 26.6 pp floor. This is a *measured near-zero with a wide interval*, not
   evidence of equality; the honest statement is "no detectable difference at n=30".
3. **The instruments are calibrated and documented, including their flaws.** Gate 75 % vs a 84 %
   human-human ceiling; a checked-in hand-read ledger of all 210 cells; a validated LLM judge
   (85 % three-way / 89 % binary on a selected panel — an upper bound; see §4); operational
   parameters and a proposed fresh-cell gate for trusting it.
4. **Data scale, not data source, is the live variable.** The one effect at the detection edge is
   v5's 93 rows vs the base (27 pp). Nine-row arms — student-state and teacher-own alike — produce
   effects under the floor. The next experiment the arithmetic supports is a dose ladder on rows
   per arm (9 / 30 / 90 vs base), not another 9-row contrast
   ([`docs/next-experiment-plan-20261014.md`](next-experiment-plan-20261014.md), Move 1).
5. **Fine-tune instability is reproducible and has a shape.** Three instances, same family:
   the 4-bit control collapse (89 `web_fetch` calls against a non-existent URL), the teacher-state
   read-skipping (0 reads in 25/30 sessions at one seed), and the holdout-4 seed0 regression
   (never lists the workspace; 96 of 109 reads fail; all 7 passes are among the 13 sessions that
   ever read). Common root: nine rows with `last` supervision teach the final answer, not the
   exploration policy — and *what the model does when a tool result is unexpected is outside the
   training signal entirely*. This is the error-recovery half of the project's own premise,
   measured and found missing.

## 4. What the evidence does not support

- **"CHTD beats the alternatives."** Not on 30 tasks (0–3 pp, floor 26.6 pp); not by hand-read
  (CHTD arms 17–19 correct of 30, the base 17, the repaired teacher-own arms 21 and 25); not in the
  blinded re-grade (CHTD seed 1 16 vs the base 13 — ordering preserved, levels lower).
- **"The adapted arms beat base" as a CHTD-specific claim.** By the corrected gate every adapted
  arm clears the base by 1–5 tasks; by hand-read the CHTD-specific arms do not. The clean
  separation belongs to v5's *volume*, not to the student-state structure.
- **"Seed-stable."** Neither the CHTD rows nor the teacher-own rows are stable at three seeds'
  distance once the read behavior is measured; holdout-4 sharpened this at a different prompt.
- **"The 85 %/89 % LLM-judge agreement licenses replacing most human reading now."** The panel is
  doubly selected (three arms, two-reader-agreed cells only); on the excluded human-disagreement
  cells the model split 9–5 and lost every argurable case in a six-cell spot-read. The documented
  fresh-cell gate (two blind readers; model-vs-human ≥ human-vs-human on the same fresh cells) is
  the missing validation, and none of the numbers above depend on it.

## 5. What a confirmatory run would need

In cost order (details and pre-registration checklist in
[`docs/next-experiment-plan-20261014.md`](next-experiment-plan-20261014.md)):

1. **Pre-registration** naming the primary metric, the read protocol, the arms and their digests,
   and the MDE implied by the planned n — the ladder at n=48 has MDE 21.3 pp; write down the step
   size it must resolve.
2. **The grading pipeline with its missing gate**: deepseek-flash first pass (max_tokens 4000,
   lenient extraction), human double-read of a stratified sample plus every disagreement, and the
   fresh-cell validation before it is trusted on new material.
3. **A dose ladder** (9 / 30 / 90 student-state rows vs base) — the question the arithmetic says is
   answerable; and record **read health per session** (the `tool-call-census.json` shape) as a
   first-class metric beside the gate, because failed exploration converts directly into gate
   failures and is invisible in the pass count.
4. **The paired question only after the slope is known** — if a dose step moves ≥20 pp, ~55
   tasks/arm can resolve it; below 10 pp nothing this project can afford will.
